Privacy policy

What data may be processed, why it is used, which providers are involved and how to exercise your rights.

Technical update

24 October 2026. Full controller identification and legal review remain pending.

1. Controller and scope

Estrecho Ormuz is owned by a natural person; full legal identification awaits real and authorised details. The project brand does not replace identification of the controller. Proposed address for privacy and rights requests: contacto@estrechoormuz.com.

Receipt at contacto@estrechoormuz.com is confirmed by the owner’s statement on 28 October 2026. Sending replies using the domain identity remains unverified; this audit has sent no messages. This technical update does not establish legal approval of controller identification or the conditions of each processing activity.

The website can be used without creating an account. This policy applies to estrechoormuz.com, its public pages, local alert features and communications linked to the project.

2. Data that may be processed

  • Technical and security data: IP address, user agent, time, requested URL, headers, errors and abuse-prevention events.
  • Aggregated usage data: visited pages, approximate referral information, load times and performance metrics.
  • Local preferences: last status seen, notification permissions and privacy choices stored by the browser or consent platform.
  • Communications: email address, message content and metadata needed when you contact the project.
  • Advertising data: once AdSense is active, Google and its partners may process identifiers, device and browser data, ad interactions and consent signals to deliver, limit, measure and protect advertising.

4. Google AdSense and Google's CMP

Google AdSense advertising is disabled. No active advertising consent message has been verified in this version. Before advertising is enabled, consent management must be configured and tested and this policy updated. Only after that authorization could Google and selected providers use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • show personalised ads where consent exists;
  • show non-personalised or limited ads where applicable;
  • measure performance, frequency and conversions;
  • prevent fraud, invalid traffic and security incidents.

A future consent platform must effectively support acceptance, rejection and changes to choices. Providers, purposes and retention periods will be displayed after configuration and testing; this page does not certify that the process has already taken place.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps on Google's Business Data Responsibility page and in its privacy policy.

5. Cloudflare, GitHub Pages and security

The domain uses Cloudflare DNS nameservers. GitHub Pages hosts and delivers the static files. Hosting and resolution providers may process technical data needed for their services; DNS delegation does not establish the use of other security or measurement products.

The audited published artifact does not include a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. Any future analytics will be documented, including provider, data, purposes and preference management.

6. Third-party content

The MyShipTracking AIS map is embedded on demand within this website. Only when the user selects the load button does the browser connect directly to MyShipTracking, which may process technical data under its own terms and privacy policy. MarineTraffic is provided only as an alternative external link.

7. Recipients, transfers and retention

Hosting and features actually used may involve processing by their providers. Each entity and its role, the destination inbox provider, retention and international transfers require confirmation. No specific contracts or transfer mechanisms have been verified or are certified by this page. Future analytics and advertising must be described before activation.

Technical logs are retained as needed for security, diagnosis and compliance. Communications are kept as long as necessary to answer and address possible liabilities. Advertising preferences and retention periods depend on the CMP, browser and provider.

8. Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or portability where applicable, and withdraw consent without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. Contact contacto@estrechoormuz.com with enough information to process the request.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

9. Children and updates

The site is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly request children's data. This policy may be updated following technical, regulatory or provider changes. The revision date will appear at the top.
