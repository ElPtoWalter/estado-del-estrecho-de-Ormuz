Accountability

Corrections policy and log

Errors are corrected visibly, proportionately and traceably. A correction is not hidden inside a silent update when it affects the conclusion.

How to request a correction

Send the exact URL, the disputed statement, a source that contradicts it and a short explanation through the contact page. Unsupported opinions can be considered but do not require a factual change.

Correction levels

LevelExampleResponse
MinorTypo, broken link or formatting.Direct fix and review date.
MaterialFigure, attribution or context changing interpretation.Visible note on the page and entry in this log.
CriticalOpen/closed status supported by invalid evidence.Immediate downgrade, new run, automated test and public explanation.

What is not erased

Status history and technical commits are retained so the evolution can be reconstructed. When a historical entry contains an error, the correction is documented and the item is prevented from acting as a current confirmation.
