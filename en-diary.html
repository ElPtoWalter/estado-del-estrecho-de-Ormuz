The Strait of Hormuz enters August 15, 2026 with an assessment of “OPEN · SEVERELY RESTRICTED TRANSIT” and medium confidence; physical passage confirmed, traffic reduced, risk extreme. Commercial transits through the strait are confirmed, but flows remain far below normal and security/access conditions are severe. It is not physically closed; it is operating on a highly constrained basis. Today's edition separates what is happening on the water from political statements and market reaction.
