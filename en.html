Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Does a political threat mean the Strait is closed?

No. The indicator separates statements, intentions and threats from a verified operational disruption.

Does the AIS map prove the Strait is open?

No. AIS is contextual and may contain gaps. It is combined with official sources, maritime notices, incidents and independent confirmations.

How often is it updated?

The observatory is continuously monitored. The time of the latest verification appears in the main panel.

Can I reuse the data?

Yes. You may cite Estrecho Ormuz's public conclusions with attribution and a link to the methodology. RSS is available for following new publications.

Responsible partnerships

Sponsorships without editorial interference

The project may accept clearly identified partnerships with organisations compatible with its informational purpose. No advertiser can alter statuses, evidence or conclusions.
