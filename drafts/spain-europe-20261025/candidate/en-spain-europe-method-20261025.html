Method, sources and rights: Spain, Europe and Hormuz

Scope and releases

Historical gas analysis for 2025, not a complete oil or energy balance or an assessment of current Hormuz conditions. Sources are the Enagás 2025 PDF and the physical-operation workbook linked on p. 80, plus the Eurostat news release of 25 March 2026 and original annex. The record identifies URLs, access date 25 October 2026 and document hashes. PDF locators use printed pages 23–25.

Reading the Enagás workbook

Sheet “Pág. 25”: E4 identifies 2025, B12 identifies Qatar, and E12 contains 0.01718890770907905 as a literal fraction, not a percentage already multiplied by 100. The workbook chart cache references the same value. We do not recalculate a nonexistent formula or use column C, from another year, as 2025.

Reconciliation verdict

Unresolved documentary discrepancy. PDF p. 25: 1.735%. Quotient from PDF pp. 23–24: 1.718935403680…%. Workbook E12 × 100: 1.718890770907905%. The latter two agree to three decimal places but do not establish the cause of the first. No specific SL-ATR reconciliation or issuer explanation was obtained. Enagás was not contacted; no such contact is claimed.

Reproducible formulas

Total share = 6403 / 372498 × 100. LNG share = 6403 / 248084 × 100. Workbook percentage = 0.01718890770907905 × 100. Percentage-point gap = 1.735 − total share. Hypothetical implied volume = 372498 × 1.735 / 100; residual = result − 6403. Implied volume is not observed supply. Decimal arithmetic avoids intermediate rounding. Eight CSV decimals aid reproduction without increasing source precision.

Checking Eurostat

VISUAL 2: A1 specifies extra-EU imports and 2025; A2 identifies trade value shares; D4 identifies LNG, D9 Qatar and E9 the 8.855309243451158% share. One-decimal rounding gives 8.9%. The news release identifies CN 27111100. The annex also contains net-mass shares in “Natural gas (liq) partners”, 2025 block A15:C19. Mass is not energy in GWh and is not silently substituted for the value share.
