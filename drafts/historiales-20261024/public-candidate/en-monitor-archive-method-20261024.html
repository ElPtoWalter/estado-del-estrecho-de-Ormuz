How to read and reproduce this archive

This method accompanies the historical analysis, not the live status. The 103 records are stored decisions from two models, not vessel positions or a periodic traffic series.

Back to the analysis

Step-by-step reproduction

  1. Download records.csv. Its six columns hold model, timestamp, classification, confidence and two boolean indicators of measurement availability; they do not contain measurements.
  2. Parse timezone-aware dates and convert to UTC. Separate legacy and v7; verify model/timestamp uniqueness. Do not add rows or fill missing days.
  3. Count rows by model, classification and confidence. Results: legacy 54 (51 INCIERTO, 3 ABIERTO); V7 49 (47 MEDIA, 2 ALTA), with the same V7 operational label in all 49 rows.
  4. Filter v7 for each window: inclusive start and exclusive end. Count rows and distinct UTC dates. Several rows on one day count as one date.
  5. First window [2026-09-10T00:00:00Z, 2026-10-01T00:00:00Z): 11 rows, 8 days, 11 MEDIA. Second [2026-10-01T00:00:00Z, 2026-10-22T00:00:00Z): 10 rows, 7 days, 10 MEDIA. Each window spans 21 days.
  6. Sort by model and timestamp. Within each model/day assign consecutive rows positions 1, 2, 3… . plot.csv preserves exact time, day and position; height only separates symbols.
  7. Draw separate model panels: X = UTC day, Y = stack position; one symbol per row, no lines or interpolation. Encode classification for legacy and confidence for V7. The bar chart represents the counts 47 and 2.
  8. Check tables against models.csv, confidence.csv and windows.csv. The two ALTA records are 7 August at 12:21:33 UTC and 2 September at 11:41:03 UTC, outside both windows. Do not calculate vessel counts, traffic declines, closure hours or accuracy.

Limitations and reuse

Days without a row prove neither an absence of ships nor service outages. The examined V7 fields contain no numeric traffic ratio or maritime-count snapshot. MEDIA and ALTA are model labels, not calibrated probabilities. Method changes and retention prevent treating both archives as one maritime series.
